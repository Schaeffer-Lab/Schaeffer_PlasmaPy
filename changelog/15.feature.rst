`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.extend_vdf_tail` replaces the tails of a PIC
distribution beyond its last macroparticle with a fitted Maxwellian, and is now
what `~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.condition_phase_space` does by default
in place of `~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.taper_vdf_edges`.

The taper was a numerical device standing in for a physical model, in the one
place the electron-plasma-wave feature actually reads. It anchored a half-cosine
to the outermost bin above a fixed fraction of the slice peak -- a criterion
with no relation to how many particles are in that bin, which on a real OSIRIS
run put the anchor at 3.24 thermal speeds while ten macroparticles reached only
2.60, so the whole tail hung off one or two particles. Its shape was wrong in
the derivative as well as the value, and Landau damping reads
:math:`\partial f/\partial v` at the resonance, so the feature's width was
fabricated with its height. Worst, the pedestal it left at large :math:`|v|`
dominates the :math:`v^2`-weighted second moment: sweeping ``max_taper_bins``
over 5, 20, 80 and unbounded moved :math:`\alpha` from 16.0 to 0.76 on the same
data, a factor of 21 on a reported plasma parameter.

The replacement finds the macroparticle quantum -- the smallest positive value
in the raw histogram is one particle -- joins at the outermost bin holding
``min_counts`` of them, and fits :math:`\ln f` against
:math:`(v - \bar{v})^2` over the resolved band with the counts as
inverse-variance weights, giving a **tail temperature**. Beyond the join the fit
takes over at its own intercept, since the join bin is a selected upward
fluctuation and anchoring there biases the tail high by 20--30%. Slices with too
little signal fall back to their own core temperature. On a sampled Maxwellian
the fitted temperature is recovered to 0.1% at :math:`10^5` particles and the
extrapolated distribution is right to 0.6% at 12 thermal speeds -- three times
beyond the last particle. Nothing is set to zero, so the positive floor is
dropped as well: it would put a flat pedestal at about 12 thermal speeds, which
is exactly where the satellite reads at :math:`\alpha \sim 12`.

Reconstructed populations, from
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.from_moments`, are skipped -- they are
already their own tail model. ``tail_model=None`` restores the taper.
