Added ``tools/pic_thomson_warpx_validation.py`` and the decks beside it in
``tools/warpx_validation_decks/``, which check
`plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson` end to end against a known answer. Each deck
is a uniform Maxwellian hydrogen plasma in 1D whose density, temperatures and
drift are set in the deck, so
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.spectral_density` gives the exact spectrum and
everything between the macroparticles and the result -- reader units and
weighting, the velocity projection, the conditioning, the tail model, the
forward model -- is under test at once. A shock has no analytic answer to check
against, so a disagreement there cannot be attributed; these can.

`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.spectra_from_phase_spaces` is also pinned
against `~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.spectral_density` over a scan in
:math:`\alpha` rather than at the single operating point
``TestMaxwellianConsistency`` used, and with metrics that mean something for a
narrow feature: integrated band power and peak position, not a pointwise ratio.
Above :math:`\alpha \sim 3` the satellites are narrow enough that half a
nanometre of offset between two otherwise correct resonances reads as a factor
of a hundred pointwise while the power under them agrees to a few percent.
