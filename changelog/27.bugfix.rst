`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.condition_phase_space` now resamples a
non-uniform velocity axis onto a uniform one, at the finest spacing the axis
has. Readers that bin proper velocity -- OSIRIS, openPMD -- return
:math:`v = u c / \sqrt{1 + u^2}`, compressed toward the edges to 0.35 of the
centre spacing on a :math:`\pm 1` grid, and
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.arbitrary_derivative` differentiates with
fixed-spacing stencils. A whole-axis velocity rescale resampled onto a uniform
grid and hid this; a species conditioned without one -- drift-only electrons, the
correct treatment for real-mass electrons -- reached the forward model raw, with
:math:`f'` wrong by up to 2.8 times. On ``omegashock_w3.5e11_exp`` that put the
EPW satellites about 6 nm from Bohm-Gross on every collective frame; they now sit
within a median of 0.12 nm (blue) and 0.37 nm (red).
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.arbitrary_derivative` now raises on a
non-uniform axis instead of returning a wrong derivative.
