Added :func:`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.spectral_density_arbitrary`
(and its :term:`lite-function`
:func:`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.spectral_density_arbitrary_lite`) to
`plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson`, which calculates the Thomson scattering
spectral density for plasmas with arbitrary (non-Maxwellian) one-dimensional
electron and ion velocity distribution functions.
