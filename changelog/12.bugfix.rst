Fixed the wavelength range a notch actually blocks in
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.spectral_density` and the three
arbitrary-distribution forward models beside it. Each located the notch
endpoints with ``argmin``, which rounds them to the nearest bin centre, and then
zeroed a half-open slice, which drops the upper endpoint bin: on the 0.4 nm grid
a synthetic OSIRIS EPW window uses, a requested ``[530, 534]`` nm came out as
``[530.2, 533.4]``. Every bin whose centre lies in the closed interval is now
zeroed, which is what a filter blocking that interval does to a binned detector.
The bin immediately outside a notch holds the skirt of the central feature,
orders of magnitude above the electron-plasma-wave satellites, so a notch
narrower than the one asked for takes most of the window once the spectrum is
normalised to unit area.
