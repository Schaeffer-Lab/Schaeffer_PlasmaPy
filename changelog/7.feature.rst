Added `plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson`, which builds synthetic Thomson
scattering spectra from particle-in-cell simulation output. This first
instalment provides the code-agnostic core: the `~plasmapy.diagnostics.
pic_thomson.PICPhaseSpace` container that PIC readers produce, and the
conditioning steps that turn raw, noisy phase space into the unit-normalised
distributions that
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.arbitrary_forwardmodel` expects --
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.smooth_vdf`,
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.taper_vdf_edges`,
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.normalize_vdf`, and
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.rescale_velocity_axis`. The driver
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.spectra_from_phase_spaces` conditions those
phase spaces, reduces them to one spatial point, and evaluates the forward model
over EPW and IAW wavelength windows at every timestep, returning a
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.ThomsonSpectrogram`. It supports several
electron and ion populations through the forward model's ``efract`` and
``ifract``, and excludes populations that are not physically present at a given
timestep rather than letting them contribute spurious signal. OSIRIS and WarpX
readers follow.
