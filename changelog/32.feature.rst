`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.spectra_from_phase_spaces` now returns
spectra on one absolute scale -- :math:`n_e S(k, \omega)`, proportional to the
scattered power -- instead of scaling every frame to unit area. The forward model
normalises each spectrum on its own, which discards the brightness history along
the time axis and the EPW-to-IAW ratio, makes a frame with no signal look like
one with a strong feature, and, where a stray-light notch has removed the central
feature, promotes whatever residue is left into a saturated rail along each notch
edge. ``normalise_each_frame=True`` restores the old behaviour, and
`~plasmapy.diagnostics.thomson.arbitrary_forwardmodel` takes ``normalize=False``
for the absolute :math:`S(k, \omega)`.

``epw_notches="auto"`` now sizes one notch for the whole run, centred on the
probe wavelength and wide enough for the central feature in its widest frame,
capped below the closest computable satellite with a warning when that binds.
It used to be re-sized every frame from a 99.9% containment interval -- set by
the wings rather than the line -- capped at a fraction of the Bohm-Gross offset,
and wandered over a factor of 19-26 in width on two runs.
