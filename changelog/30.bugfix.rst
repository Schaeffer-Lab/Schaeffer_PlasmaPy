`~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.extend_vdf_tail` no longer assumes the
smallest positive value of a histogram is one macroparticle unless the
histogram really is whole multiples of it. OSIRIS deposits fractional, variably
weighted contributions; there the smallest value is a sliver of a particle, and
on ``omegashock_w3.5e11_exp`` the inferred count was 12-14 times the deck's
(5-41 interquartile), so ``min_counts=10`` meant about one particle and the tail
join sat in noise. For such slices the quantum now comes from the slice's own
shot noise -- bin variance is ``quantum * f``, measured from second differences
and corrected for the correlation a shaped deposit puts between neighbouring
bins -- and never claims more particles than the smallest value allows. Against
the deck's macroparticle count it lands at 0.84-1.07 for ions and about 1.9 for
electrons. Quantised histograms (WarpX) are unchanged. ``quantum`` (and
``tail_quantum`` on `~plasmapy.diagnostics.pic_thomson.condition_phase_space`)
overrides the estimate when the weights are known.
