``tools/pic_thomson_osiris_spectra.py`` draws ``media/14_osiris_spectra.png``:
the EPW and IAW spectrograms of an OSIRIS run under the reduced-mass-ratio
corrections, with no legacy column.

Each panel uses **one absolute colour scale across every timestep**. Rescaling
each row to its own maximum -- what the other spectrogram figures do -- makes a
frame carrying no signal indistinguishable from one carrying a strong feature,
and where a stray-light notch has removed the central feature the only content
left is the skirt at the notch edge, a factor of :math:`10^{-5}` below the peak,
which the renormalisation promotes into a saturated rail along each notch edge
across every such frame.
