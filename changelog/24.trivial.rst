``tools/pic_thomson_osiris_comparison.py`` no longer sizes its stray-light mask
from the spectrum each frame. A notch is a piece of glass and does not change
width between frames; the automatic sizing ranged over 0.52 to 9.92 nm on
``omegashock_w3.5e11_exp``, a factor of 19, because it measures a 99.9%
containment interval -- set by whatever is in the wings rather than by the line
-- and then caps it at a fraction of the Bohm-Gross offset, which makes it a
function of density. ``--auto-notch`` still selects the old behaviour.

The colour limit on the spectrograms is now taken from outside
``--colour-guard`` nanometres of the probe line. The central feature is orders
of magnitude above the satellites, so a limit set from the whole panel puts
every satellite in the bottom colour: on this run it hid the legacy
configuration's satellites at 480 and 580 nm entirely, and they were always in
the data.

``--probe-cells`` averages the phase space over the cells a real collection
volume spans, defaulting to 5 -- 100 microns here, against a 19.9 micron cell.

``tools/pic_thomson_kinshock.py`` is new, and makes ``media/13_kinshock_470eV.png``
reproducible; it had been generated by hand.
